How do you want to fly?

Two sticks: hold the phone sideways. Mickey Mode: hold it upright like a DJI motion controller and point it at the game — a cursor shows where you're pointing and the drone turns toward it. Point above the horizon to climb, below to dive, straight ahead to fly straight; THROTTLE makes it go, the thumb pad strafes and trims height, RE-CENTER makes where you're pointing the new straight ahead. You can switch any time while flying.
