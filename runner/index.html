The game ships no music. Demo mode uses its own click grid; file mode grades tricks against the BPM grid above, starting when the song starts.

How to fly

Swipe ◀ ▶ change lane (with a roll) · Swipe ▲ frontflip + pop up · Swipe ▼ backflip + dive · Double-tap barrel roll · Press & hold spin — release to finish the trick. Desktop: arrow keys / WASD, hold Space to spin, P pauses.
Barriers block a whole lane — move over. Low tables — pop up / frontflip over them. Hanging banners — dive under with a backflip.
Tricks score when they complete on the beat: the ONE pays 350, the THREE 325, any beat 300, the off-beat "ands" pay 200 SYNCOPATED. No misses — off the grid is just FREE.
