How do you want to fly?

Two sticks: hold the phone sideways. Mickey Mode: hold it upright like a DJI motion controller and aim the back of the phone at the game — the camera looks where you point (up, down, left, right) and THROTTLE flies you that way, faster the further you push it. Point past the edge to keep turning; RE-CENTER makes where you're pointing the new straight ahead; SENSITIVITY sets how far the camera moves for a move of the phone. You can switch any time while flying.
The phone always flies in Easy Mode. The selector picks ACRO (one-button tricks) or ZOOM·PITCH (hold to tilt and zoom the Avata 360's camera).
